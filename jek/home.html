Our Story

From Dhaka to
Knoxville with Love

প্রাচুর্যের রসুইঘর was born from a deep desire to share the warmth of Bangladeshi home cooking with the Knoxville community. What started as cooking for friends and family gatherings grew into something much more — a kitchen that brings the real tastes of Bangladesh to Tennessee.


We specialize in pre-order catering, preparing everything fresh so your guests experience each dish at its absolute best. Whether it's a small family dinner or a celebration for a hundred, we bring the same care and tradition to every tray.
